Studio

A small music workstation. Build a drum pattern, write a melody on the piano roll, balance everything on the mixer and download your loop as a WAV. Every sound is made live in your browser, and your work is remembered here. Tick Sound-effect mode to make short sounds: slide and fade notes in the Piano roll, then export one trimmed take.

The programme

  1. Practise at the keyboard

    Pick any chord, scale or progression, then see it light up and hear it played. Repeat until your hands know it.

    Chords as letters, six chords as words, progressions as sentences. Lessons that are hard to find on your own.

    Theory is the shortcut. Once you see the pattern, a song in any key is the same song.
